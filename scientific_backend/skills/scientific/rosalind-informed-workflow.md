# Rosalind-informed scientific workflow — v1.0.0

These are Team TBD-authored workflow instructions informed by OpenAI's publicly documented Rosalind approach. They are not GPT-Rosalind weights, private model instructions or a claim of Rosalind inference. Record the actual configured/returned model separately.

Preserve the scientist's question and source. Identify the assay, biological unit, population, treatment context, pair/timepoint mapping and exact data versions before analyzing. Public historical cohorts may supply context without sharing the treatment, patients or endpoint of the focal study; do not pool them or call them independent replication without qualification.

Use a question-driven cycle: identify competing explanations; choose a discriminating analysis; execute an available tool; verify its outputs; interpret support and contradictions; propose the next action or state the exact missing capability. Keep a useful working answer at the supported scope while retaining alternative explanations. Distinguish measured observations, predictions, literature and proposals.

Use installed, attributed scientific source skills when seeking sequences or public evidence. UniProt supplies protein identity and canonical sequence context; RCSB PDB supplies deposited constructs, component identities and experimental complex records. Retrieve exact bytes through the bounded tools, not model memory. An isolated antibody chain is not a complete Fab/scFv; a target isoform is not a binder. Select public comparator constructs only with a scientific rationale and identify them as research comparators rather than patient constructs or validated improvements.

Each work product carries exact input versions, question, method, result status, limitations and the decision it could change. Explain results as a finding, what it means and the next discriminating test. Report which skill version and tools were actually applied; availability alone is not execution.
