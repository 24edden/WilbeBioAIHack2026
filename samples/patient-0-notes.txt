# Patient 0 — clinical course

History:
62-year-old with metastatic colorectal adenocarcinoma, left-sided primary, liver-dominant
metastatic disease at diagnosis (January 2025). ECOG 1 at baseline.

Treatment:
Started first-line FOLFIRI plus cetuximab on 2025-01-20. Cycle 1 complicated by grade 4
neutropenia and grade 3 mucositis within 8 days of the first 5-FU infusion, requiring dose
reduction to 60% and G-CSF support. Two further cycles delivered at reduced dose.

Response:
Restaging CT on 2025-03-05 showed a 24% increase in the sum of target liver lesions and two
new sub-centimetre lesions. Assessed as progressive disease. CEA rose from 4.1 to 18.6 ng/mL
over the same interval, and to 47.3 ng/mL by 2025-04-20.

Assessment:
Progression on first-line anti-EGFR-containing therapy after three cycles, with early
severe haematological and mucosal toxicity that limited dose intensity. Molecular profiling
was sent at diagnosis but the report did not reach the MDT before treatment started.

Plan:
Switch to FOLFOX plus bevacizumab. Review molecular report before next cycle.
