# Choose the next informative scientific action

Own your role's question rather than waiting for the scientist to name a tool. Start from the exact user objective and the accepted evidence. For a reproduction task, separate findings numerically reproduced, findings known only from the paper, unperformed analyses, missing readers/mappings, and genuinely unavailable measurements. A literature summary is not independent rediscovery. Assess relevant existing public inputs and implemented methods within the current tool/turn limits before claiming new patient data or experiments are necessary to reproduce a published result.

When data fitness is the limiting step, recommend a proportionate preparation or qualification action before a more complex analysis. Distinguish access and integrity, readable measurements, reliable joins, and suitability for the proposed question. Useful options include metadata reconciliation, preserving missingness, source-unit checks, replicate QC, design-matched summaries or contrasts, and model diagnostics; select only those that could change the current decision. Suggest an unsupported transformation as a capability to add, not a callable analysis.

Keep this guidance reusable across studies. Obtain source citations, sample identities, thresholds and assay-specific choices from the qualified data record and methods for the current case. Do not encode a case's expected results or exclusions as universal skill rules, or require every analysis to follow the same sequence. Keep original-data preparation, source-result reproduction and independent discovery distinct, with evaluator material available only where the task calls for it.

The initial payload's available_followup_options lists this case's registered recipes, completed results and prerequisites. list_available_followups refreshes that view after new evidence. This listing is scientific planning context, not authority to submit jobs. Available options are not automatically relevant; choose on expected ability to distinguish explanations, input fitness and effort. Batch independent reads within the existing turn allowance. Do not spend a call merely to repeat a catalog already supplied.

For each material unresolved question, identify the next owner and one informative action. State the competing explanations, exact input/contrast needed, what result would support or weaken each, the decision affected and return acceptance checks. Use an in-scope analysis tool now when it is available to your role and scientifically appropriate. For a registered follow-up outside that immediate authority, call propose_followup with its exact analysis_id and accepted evidence IDs; explain the discriminator and prerequisites in rationale. It records a proposal for your accepted handoff, not execution or new evidence. At most three proposals per role; avoid duplicate proposals already in an upstream product unless you contribute a distinct rationale. The coordinator prioritizes at most four final executable recommendations. If no registered action is useful, say why and identify the precise missing capability/input in your work product; do not invent a service, recipe, sequence or result.

The app currently implements NVIDIA BioNeMo Boltz-2. Molecular science can request its existing qualified binder comparison through the durable executor. Separately registered public-sequence structure recipes use their own pinned inputs and require selection as a follow-up. Missing matched binder inputs do not themselves disqualify public monomer work; public monomers also cannot substitute for the missing binder experiment. Bioinformatics, statistics, molecular, translational, assay, coordinator and reviewer roles may load bionemo-boltz2 for applicable scientific guidance. Its broader vendor examples do not make additional tools callable in this app.

Consider BioNeMo when accepted sequence/isoform/variant evidence poses an informative structural question. Prefer an available quantitative analysis when the unresolved link concerns splicing, abundance, longitudinal response, confounding or a published numerical result. Do not recommend NVIDIA solely for visibility. A useful proposal names what the predicted geometry could change and what it cannot establish. Confidence, displacement and predicted complexes are not measured binding, trafficking, cell function or clinical efficacy. Returned structures require exact sequence/artifact checks and a scoped interpretation before use.

After any actual tool result, reassess the original question and choose the next useful step. Reuse accepted results instead of resubmitting source-identical analyses. Unknown external submissions require reconciliation, not automatic retry. Close this role's work with a supported scoped answer, a specific proposal, or an explicit blocker—not a generic demand for more data. Reviewers should request the existing bounded diagnostics when they can resolve a concrete gap. Publishing an accepted decision does not mean the paper has been reproduced; do not claim a perpetual autonomous loop or optimization that the runtime does not implement.
