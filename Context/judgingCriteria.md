# Judging

Single-track, judged hackathon. Each team delivers a **five-minute presentation on Sunday**
showcasing its work.

**Panel:** six judges from **NVIDIA**, **OpenAI**, and **Amino Collective**.
**Scoring:** each judge independently scores every project **1–10** on **5 equally
weighted criteria**. Scores guide a discussion; judges have final decision over prizes.

## Criteria (5 × equal weight, 1–10 each)

### 1. Scientific relevance and impact
- Does the project address a meaningful problem in science, healthcare, or life sciences?
- Is the proposed workflow **meaningfully better than the current alternative**?

### 2. Effective application of NVIDIA and OpenAI technology
- Does the project use NVIDIA and OpenAI technologies in a meaningful way?
- Are those technologies **central to the solution rather than incidental**?

### 3. Execution
- How well does the implementation work?
- Is the system thoughtfully designed and **sufficiently complete to demonstrate the core idea**?

### 4. Originality
- Is the approach unique or novel, and distinct from what other teams worked on?
- Is it meaningfully different from existing approaches?

### 5. Presentation & Reproducibility
- How well does the presentation reflect the criteria above?
- Does it include a **functioning demo** or other convincing evidence?
- Could **another team reproduce the key results** from the submitted materials and repository?

## Submissions

Required, in addition to the oral presentation:

1. A **Google Slides presentation** using the official Submission Template.
2. Access to a **single GitHub repository** containing all code needed to reproduce the
   results or demos shown in the presentation.

## Prizes

Awarded per team; the team decides how to split them.

| Place | Prize |
|-------|-------|
| 1st | $5,000 Brev Credits + $5,000 Codex Credits |
| 2nd | $3,000 Brev Credits + $3,000 Codex Credits |
| 3rd | $1,500 Brev Credits + $1,500 Codex Credits |

## Sharing results

Sharing presentation results publicly is encouraged (guidance will be provided), and
organizers would like to amplify social posts afterwards. **None of this is required to
win a prize.**
